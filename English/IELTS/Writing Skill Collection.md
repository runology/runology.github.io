# Writing Task 2

TYPES:

- Opinion *(Easiest)*
- Cause & Effect （discussion essay）
- Compare & Contrast
- Problem & Solution
- **Argumentative Essay *(Most Common)***
    - Agree
    - Advantage
    - Discuss Both Views & Give your own Idea

## Task Requirement

- something general in society:
    - details of an opinion
    - an issue or a problem that you have to write an essay in response
- Topics
    
    Education ,  Environment, Healthcare, Gender roles,  Poverty
    
- At least 250 words

## Structure of Essay

## Introduction

- 2 - 3 sentences
    - Hook (Can be absent): very short. *e.g, Distractions will not help students.*
    - General statement Or Background Info: 1 Sentence: Paraphrase the question.
    - Thesis Statement: Your 2 opinions
    
    Sentence 2: Your stance / voice.
    

## Body Content

<aside>
💡 Each body 2-5 sentences
Only 2 Bodies is the best.

</aside>

### Body structure:

- Topic Sentence: 1 sentence about one of your opinions in other words
- Give details: explaining your idea. **2-3 sentences**. How and why?
- Examples: be specific (use name)
    - An example of this can be seen in xxx.
    - For example

### Body content for different Types

- 1: **Opinion** Essay: Positive or Negative opinion for each body
    
    <aside>
    ❓ Some people say university students should pay for their tuition fees. Some others say the government should pay for the tuition fees. What is your opinion?
    
    </aside>
    
    Do not mix the positive or negative Attitudes, You must choose one.
    
    - body 1, negative side 1
    - body 2, positive side 1
    - body 3, positive side 2
- 2: **Cause & Effec**t Essay
    
    <aside>
    ❓ “Global warming has had a profound impact on the environment. Discuss the main causes of global warming and its specific effects on the natural environment and human life.”
    
    </aside>
    
    Possible Structure: cause+cause+effect, cause +effect+effect
    
    - body 1, cause
    - body 2, effect 1
    - body 3, effect 2
- 3: **Compare & Contrast** Essay
    
    <aside>
    ❓ “Some people believe that university education should focus mainly on academic knowledge, while others argue that it should include vocational training. Compare and contrast these two viewpoints and discuss which one you think is more important.”
    
    </aside>
    
    Similarities and differences
    
    - body1, similarity 1
    - body2, diff 1
    - body3, diff 2
- 4: **Problem & Solution** Essay
    
    <aside>
    ❓ “Urbanization has led to many problems in cities, such as traffic congestion and environmental pollution. What are the main causes of these problems? What measures can governments and individuals take to address them?”
    
    </aside>
    
    - body 1, problem causes
    - body 2, solution 1
    - body 3, solution 2
- 5: **Argumentative** Essay  （Most Common）
    - 5-1: Advantages and Disadvantages
        - positive opinion, like immigration,  structure: b1-disadvantage1, b2-advantage1, b3-advantage2
        - negative opinion, vice versa
    - 5-2: Agree and Disagree (80%)
        - disagree, b1-agree1, b2-disagree1, b3-disagree2
        - agree, b1-disagree, b2-agree1, b3-agree2
    - 5-3: Discuss both views & give your Opinion
        
        方案一: 三段
        
        - body1, some people
        - body2, others
        - your own opinion
        
        方案二: 两段 (推荐)
        
        - body 1, opposite opinion
            - 别人认为,期望….,  用这样的语气来为下一段展开自己的观点做铺垫.
                - government being expected to pay for medical care
                - it is understandable, 因为xx原因, 他们这样认为可以理解, 引出下段转折
        - body 2, your opinion

## Conclusion

- summary: 1 sentence
    
    S1: review the main bodies
    
    start by “In conclusion”, “To sum up”, then paraphrase your thesis statement.
    
- suggestion or prediction (optional): 1 sentence

### How many words in each paragraph?

- introduction: 40 words
- body 1: 100 words
- body 2: 100 words
- conclusion: 30-35 words

another form of bodies:

- body 1: 60-70 words
- body2: 60-70 words
- body3: 60-70words.

body should have the same length

# Band Descriptors for Task 1

### What is Band Descriptor

<aside>
❓ A band descriptor is a guideline used by examiners when marking writing.

</aside>

assessing your writing

can help you to make sure you **include everything you need to maximum your score.**

4 Criteria for Task 1:

- Task achievement
- Coherence and Cohesion
- Lexical Resource (Vocabulary)
- Grammatical Range and Accuracy

Don’t neglect any one criterion, and these criteria then break down further to what was known as the band descriptors.

### C1: Task Achievement

Requirements: 

- Fully **address** the question/task.
- Illustrate the key **features** and give a fully developed **response**.
- Have an **overview** that highlights the key features
- Ensure your report is **accurate**
- Make **comparisons** where relevant
- Do not express an opinion
- More than 150 words

Key Differences between band scores:

Band 6 - some details may be inappropriate or inaccurate

Band 7 - some features might need to be developed more.

Band 8 - covers the task sufficiently and illustrates key features.

Band 9 - fully satisfies all requirements of taks and gives a fully developed answer.

### C2: Coherence and Cohesion

coherence means logic among ideas

cohesion means logic among sentences

Requirements:

- **Paraphrase** effectively with no loss of meaning
- **Organize** and group ideas in a logical way
- Use **paragraphing** effectively （4 Paragraphs）
- Use **cohesive devices** effectively and accurately （Linking words）
- Use **referencing** language appropriately （e.g. respectively, this, it）

Key Differences between band scores

Band 6 - may not use referencing language **or** errors in linking within and between sentences

Band 7 - may have under or over use of cohesive devices.

Band 8 - uses paragraphing appropriately, sequences effectively, has clear organization and manages cohesion well

Band 9 - the ideas tie together smoothly, cohesion is used naturally and paragraphing is used skillfully.

## C3: Lexical Resource

AWL: academic word list

Requirements:

- use a **sufficient** range of vocabulary
- use vocabulary **accurately**
- make only **rare** errors in spelling and words formation
- use **less common** vocabulary skillfully
- **collocate** correctly （use words with other words correctly）
- don’t use **informal** language or **abbreviations**

Key Differences 

Band 6 - adequate, but some less common vocabulary is inaccurate, some spelling errors

Band 7 - sufficient, including some less common voca, allow occasional errors in spelling

Band 8 - wide , precise, occasional inaccuracies in collocation and rare spelling errors

Band 9 - wide, errors are rare and only due to occasional slips (并不是不会写只是不小心手滑犯错)

## C4: Grammatical range and accuracy

Passive Voice more than 6

Requirements:

- wide range of sentence structure
- a variety of **complex structures**
- avoid mistakes
- **appropriate tense** for the question
- correct **punctuation**

Key differences:

Band 6 - mix of simple and complex sentences, errors in grammar and punctuation

Band 7 - mix of complex structures, and good control of grammar and punctuation, allow a few errors

Band 8 - wide range of structures, most are error free

Band 9 - wide range of structures and errors are very rare.

# 8 steps to help you reach a band 8 in task 2

### Task Response

- sufficiently address all parts of the task
- presents a well-developed response to the question with **relevant**, extend and supported ideas

- **#1 Answer the Question relevantly**
    
    **`DO`:** 
    
    - Make sure your ideas are directly related to the question
    - Use ideas and examples that you are familiar with that relate to the topic directly
    - Extend your answer to include a number of ideas that will support the question relevantly
    
    **`Don't`:** 
    
    - don’t include irrelevant information (删掉不相关的东西)
    - don’t over-generalize (以偏概全)
    - don’t produce a learned-off essay (提前背诵的)
    - don’t present ‘recent’ research or statistics related to the topic :
        
        **‘at least 41% of all men …’** (雅思写作没有时间和机会调研数据，你提供的数据没有准确度)
        
- **#2 Answer All Parts of the question**
    
    **`DO`:** 
    
    - Read the question carefully and decide how many parts are in it.
    - Present your opinion and support it throughout the whole essay
    - If asked to present both views, make sure each view is presented equally (similar paragraph length)
    - Watch for plurals. If you are asked to give advantages you must present a minimum of **2.**
    - Watch for the word ‘**and’** in the [premis](http://premis.you)e. You may need to comment on more than element.
    
    **`Don't`:** 
    
    - Don’t ignore parts of the question
    - never use: I, We, You, us, our
    - Don’t Tell the examiner what you are going to say and what you have already said (informal)

### Coherence and Cohesion

- sequences information and ideas logically
- manages all aspects of cohesion well
- uses paragraphing sufficiently and appropriately

- **#3 Organize you essay Logically**
    
    **`DO`:** 
    
    - use a range of linking words and phrases, but **don’t overuse**
    - use adverbial phrases, rather than single basic linkers
    - use **referencing** and substitution to avoid repetition
    - use Punctuation to make your writing coherent
    
    **`Don't`:** 
    
    - don’t start every sentence with a linker. try to put it in the **middle**
    - don’t use numbers, symbols or abbreviations
    - don’t use headings or subheadings
- **#4 Organize into Paragraphs**
    
    Each paragraph contains a clear topic that is developed.
    
    A paragraph usually must have a minimum of 2 sentences
    
    **PEEL**
    
    **Point** - Introduce your topic sentence
    
    **Example** - example that supports your point
    
    **Explain** - Why this evidence supports your point
    
    **Link** - Transition to the next topic/paragraph
    
    **`DO`:** 
    
    - use linkers between and within paragraphs
    - Leave a space between each paragraph (a line)
    - use a paragraph for each topic
    - use an introduction and a conclusion
    
    **`Don't`:** 
    
    - don’t use single sentence paragraphs
    - don’t use very long paragraphs
    - don’t underuse paragraphs, more than 3

### Lexical Resource

- uses a wide range of vocabulary fluently and flexibly to convey precise meanings
- skillfully uses uncommon lexical items but there may be occasional inaccuracies in word choice and collocation.
- produce rare errors in spelling and word formation

- **#5 use less common vocabulary**
    
    **Common Terms** are words and phrases we use every day to refer to personal experience and daily habits.
    
    **Uncommon Term**s are used when we discuss specific topics or when we use idiomatic language.
    
    Words that are **old-fashioned** and not used in everyday speech should not be used.
    
    Synonym: The meaning mustn’t alter the idea. e.g., adolescent and teenagers, ~~toddler/baby~~
    
    **Collocation**
    
    idioms should only be used when you understand them completely and they fit the topic. for example:
    
    throw out/away = discard
    
    throw up = vomit/get sick
    
    **`DO`:** 
    
    - be **precise** with your words
    - use language that is used in everyday speech
    - use words that you understand
    - use words and phrase that are related to the topic
    - use collocation and phrasal verbs (正确搭配)
    
    **`Don't`:** 
    
    spelling mistakes, typos(手滑打错), 
    
    Mix up US and UK spelling, 
    
    use **imprecise** words, like ~~stuff, thing~~
    
    use slang like ‘gonna’
    
    use old-fashioned language: ~~hitherto | myopic view | the masses | denizens~~
    
    Overuse synonyms, one is enough
    
    use idioms
    
    use contractions ~~(can’t, doesn’t)~~
    
- **#6 language shouldn’t be used**
    
    **Idioms** are generally spoken and not used when writing essays.
    
    **Proverbs** and cliches should also be avoided, as they are vague and do not address a task.
    
    Use **clear** language

## P5：Line Graphs Questions

- multiple lines
- Changes over time
- Similar trends/values

### General Guide

- Analyze the graph and plan how tow group the information
    - Organize the info into 2 categories
    - Describe key info from each category
- Write an essay using the recommended essay structure.
