### Introduction:

![Untitled](../_images/Writing%20Compare%20&%20Contrast%20272a293c3b72432a97f25b75b7061400/Untitled%201.png)

- Definition: any unfamiliar terms in the title
- background information
- aim and purpose
    
    **e.g.,** *The purpose of this paper is to investigate changes in the incidence of extreme warm
    and cold temperatures over the globe since 1870.*
    
- mention of sources you have read on the topic
    
    *e.g., There is no clear empirical evidence sustaining a ‘managerial myopia’ argument. Pugh et
    al. (1992) find evidence that supports such a theory, but Meulbrook et al. (1990), Mahoney
    et al. (1997), Garvey and Hanka (1999) and a study by the Office of the Chief Economist of
    the Securities and Exchange Commission (1985) find no evidence.*
    
- limitation
    
    e.g., *This study will focus on mergers in the media business between 1995 and 2010, since
    with more recent examples an accurate assessment of the consequences cannot yet be made.*
    
- hook
    - quotation
    - a little known or striking fact
    - opinion you intend to challenge
    - explanation of why the topic is worth writing/reading
    - a question

### Body Paragraphs

1. topic sentence
2. explain your topic sentence if needed
3. introduce your evidence
    1. support your idea
    2. comes from credible source, books or papers
4. unpack (explain) your evidence. 
    1. make a connection between your point and the evidence.
5. concluding sentence

# Criteria (Of Point-by-point)

### Introduction

![](https://cdn.nlark.com/yuque/0/2024/png/295096/1715149747270-9597a9e1-c582-4f81-86c4-41e542b1c600.png)

Form:

- General Statement: 1-2 sentences
- Thesis Statement: write the similarities and diffs

Tips:

- never use I , we in this part.
- 在 Thesis Statement 中， 每出现一个相同点或不同点， 在 MainBody 中，就需要有一个段落来逐步阐明这个观点。

### Main Body

![](https://cdn.nlark.com/yuque/0/2024/png/295096/1715150221838-4cff06cd-c89a-4077-aee9-6c598e9ebd42.png)

FORM:

- Topic Sentence: 1 句 only
- Support Sentences: 2-9 句

TIPS:

- Main Body is the most important part, and the first step to write
- Write the similarity first, then the difference.
- Never use I, you, we
