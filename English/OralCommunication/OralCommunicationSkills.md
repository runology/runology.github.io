### Improve Communication

- Be an active listener
- give your full attention
- Take notes
- Ask for clarification
- Use effective clarification strategies by restating the speaker’s points and asking clarifying questions

# Clarify Questions

- paraphrase the idea
- ask related question

What did you mean by … ?

When you said…, did you mean…?

I’m not sure I followed your idea about …, can you explain what you meant?
Could you explain what you meant by … ?

What’s that supposed to mean? (maybe impolite)

More example of signposts for digression

- "Before we move on, let's briefly explore..."
• "Now, let's take a moment to discuss..."
• "This reminds me of an interesting point related to our main topic: ..."
• "While this may be slightly off-topic, it's worth mentioning that..."
• "I'd like to pause here and delve into a related subject: ..."
• "Before returning to our main focus, consider this side note..."
• "Going back to our main idea, let's now examine..."
• "Though it's not directly relevant, it's worth noting that..."
• "To digress for a moment, let's consider..."
• "Although this is a tangent, it's essential to mention that..."

More Example of Signals for a return to topic

• Let's revisit...
• Going back to...
• Regarding [previous topic]...
• Back on the subject of...
• Picking up from where we left off...
• Returning to the point about...
• Continuing with [previous topic]...
• Speaking of [previous topic]...
• Now, getting back to...
• I'd like to circle back to...
• Now, to go back to what we discussed earlier...
• To follow up on our previous discussion about...
• Revisiting the topic of...
• Regarding the issue of...
• Re-approaching the subject of...
• With respect to our earlier conversation about...
• Let's not forget about...
• I'd like to expand on the point we made earlier about...
• This connects to what we were talking about earlier, which was..
